Lu Shen

I study atmospheric chemistry and its connections to the climate system.

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences, School of Physics, at Peking University. My research focuses on methane, hydroxyl radicals, and air pollutants, and the mechanistic links among these species and their feedback on climate.

I combine atmospheric chemistry modeling and satellite remote sensing, with an emphasis on advancing both methodologies.
